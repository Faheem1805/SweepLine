# AI-use declaration

We used AI tools in this project, chiefly Claude for the simulation and app code, the studies, the poster build and reviews of the design.

The AI also got things wrong, and we caught them by checking the numbers against the physics: an early power figure left out the lift over the breakwater, and an early claim about the float-line relief did not hold up in the model. Those corrections are listed on the Evidence page.

Numbers come from the simulation and studies in this repository, not from an AI's say-so. The team is responsible for all of it.
